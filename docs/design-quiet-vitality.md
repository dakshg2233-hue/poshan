# Poshan, Reference-Led Design Specification

## Ground-Truth Reference

The supplied template defines a **single, full-viewport wellness-product hero**: a moody image-led canvas, huge elegant wordmark, understated frosted navigation, a subtle technical grid, and a lower-screen cursor spotlight which reveals product motion. Poshan will preserve this visual hierarchy and interaction language, while shifting the story from a wearable device to an intelligent daily nutrition companion.

## Chosen Approach, Quiet Vitality

### Design Movement

Contemporary editorial wellness with a **dark botanical still-life** treatment. The interface uses the tension between a rich, low-key food photograph and precise, almost scientific interface details.

### Core Principles

The product image remains the emotional centre. Typography is monumental yet restrained. Every control is nearly weightless, letting the content breathe. The cursor reveal is purposeful: it uncovers vitality in motion rather than becoming decorative spectacle.

### Color Philosophy

Charcoal-black creates visual quiet and lets warm, nourishing tones (fig brown, papaya, saffron, and leaf green) feel vivid but natural. White typography is used sparingly for crystalline legibility. **Poshan Leaf**, a fresh muted green, is the signature signal for availability and action.

### Layout Paradigm

A composition-first, layered full-screen stage replaces a conventional section stack. The logo anchors the left edge, a floating navigation ribbon hangs across the upper centre, and editorial display type cuts across the image plane.

### Signature Elements

Poshan uses a soft technical corner-grid, liquid-glass pills with luminous hairline edges, and a low, feathered spotlight that uncovers live movement only in the lower visual field.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions are tactile and understated. Navigation pills brighten slightly on hover; buttons compress subtly on press. Pointer motion feels physical through eased parallax and a delayed, soft-edged light pool.

### Animation

The grid and cursor spotlight interpolate with smooth 60fps motion. The mobile menu enters with a short upward stagger and closes immediately. All motion respects reduced-motion preferences and stays beneath 300ms except the ambient pointer interpolation.

### Typography System

Instrument Serif carries the display wordmark in uppercase, with an intentionally high-contrast editorial presence. DM Sans is used for labels, navigation, and supporting copy, balancing human warmth and clean utility. Large display type has tight leading; interface text stays compact and moderately spaced.

### Brand Essence

**Poshan is a quietly intelligent nutrition companion for people who want daily nourishment to feel considered, personal, and easy.** Its personality is **nourishing, lucid, and composed**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines feel concise and assured; CTAs invite a calm next step rather than creating urgency. Example lines: “Nourishment, in your rhythm.” and “Build your everyday balance.” Generic welcome language and exaggerated health promises are excluded.

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is rendered as a poised editorial monogram. The icon is a bold, text-free four-part botanical kernel: an abstract seed formed by interlocking leaves that also evokes a nutrition plate viewed from above.

### Signature Brand Color

**Poshan Leaf, #8FBF72**

## Style Decisions

The hero must consistently depict a dark botanical nutrition still-life with recognisable organic ingredients, warm edible colour, tactile texture, and vitality. Poshan Leaf is the sole action and status accent, applied visibly to primary actions and a small number of purposeful brand signals. The Instrument Serif wordmark and the text-free four-part botanical kernel icon together form the repeatable Poshan signature.
